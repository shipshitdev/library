# Epistemics

Confidence tiers for `why` synthesis. The synthesizer must follow this
phrasing. Dropping hedges to sound authoritative is the failure mode.

## Tiers

- **Direct evidence.** The source states the intent in so many words.
  Present tense. Quote or close paraphrase. Cite the commit, PR, ticket,
  doc, or permalink.
- **Supported inference.** A and B together make C likely. Use
  "appears to", "likely", "suggests". Spell the chain.
- **Hypothesis.** The record fits more than one story. List them with
  evidence for and against. Do not force a winner.
- **Gap.** You looked and found nothing, or you could not look. Say
  exactly what you searched.

## Rules

- A bullet with no citation is inference or a hypothesis, not a finding.
- Citing the code as evidence for its own intent is mechanics, not
  motivation.
- The most recent commit is not automatically authoritative.
- If the user suggests a reason, treat it as a hypothesis and check it.
