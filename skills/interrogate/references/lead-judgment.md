# Lead judgment

Reviewers see a slice. You have the goal, the constraints, and the
tradeoffs already considered. Use that context.

- **Act on.** Correctness, security, or maintainability issues that
  would block a real PR given the actual goals.
- **Consider.** Legitimate, but cost/benefit is unclear right now.
- **Noted.** Technically valid, not actionable at this stage.
- **Dismissed.** Wrong, nitpicky, or missing context. Say why so the
  human can override you.

Do not average reviewers. Agreement raises confidence. A lone finding
can still be Act on when you can confirm it in the code.
